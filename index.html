Engagement Brief

Organizational Autopsy™ — Acme Holdings. June – August 2026.

Engagement Summary

Organizational Autopsy™ is a 12-week executive diagnosis designed to identify structural failure points, decision pathologies, and governance drift before they become intractable.

We work from evidence, not opinion. Interviews, artifact analysis, and decision mapping produce a calibrated view of how work actually moves — and where it stalls.

Scope

  • Executive interviews across Group leadership and two divisional cohorts
  • Decision-rights mapping and operating rhythm audit
  • Structural Governance Review — Board pack and recommendations
  • Organizational Autopsy — Interim and Final Findings
  • Redesign options, transition plan, and implementation scaffolding

Out of scope: executive coaching, individual performance assessment, compensation design.

All deliverables are confidential to Acme Holdings and the engagement team. Board circulation only unless otherwise agreed in writing.
